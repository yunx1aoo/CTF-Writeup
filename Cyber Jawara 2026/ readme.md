# Cyber Jawara 2026 - Writeup

Cyber Jawara, meaning "Cyber Champion" in Indonesian, is a premier annual cybersecurity competition held in Indonesia since 2012, designed to identify and develop national talent in digital security. It features Jeopardy-style and Attack & Defense Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges covering ethical hacking, forensics, and network defense
